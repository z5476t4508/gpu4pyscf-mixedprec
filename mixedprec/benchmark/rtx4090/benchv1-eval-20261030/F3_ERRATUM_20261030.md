## 1. Original F3 contract and its standing verdict: FAIL

The pre-run pass criteria in this card fixed, before any measurement:
|dE| <= 1e-8 Ha and back-rotated max|dg| <= 3e-5 ("invariance tolerances above
met on both geometries").

- Attempt 1 (retained as `*.attempt1`): test-fixture defect — the hand-typed
  rotation literal was not orthonormal (row norms ~1.0016, off-diagonal ~0.05);
  dE 7.5156e-3 was the physics of a deformed geometry. It is a fixture error,
  not the F3 scientific result, and it does not explain attempt 2.
- Attempt 2 (valid rotation, matrix computed from Rz(.3)Ry(.7)Rx(1.1)):
  max|dE| = 5.437911681838159e-06 and max|dg| (back-rotated) =
  8.598941960720474e-05; both runs rc=0, converged (14 cycles).
  `f3_rigid_verdict.json` records **verdict: FAIL**.

**F3 final status: FAIL / unresolved under the original pre-registered
contract.** The earlier summary prose in this card ("F3 ... PASS on
tripwires", "All three families green") and in the PHASE_RECORD heading
("GPU regression (all-green ...)") is superseded by this erratum. F1 (host-DFJK

## 2. F3b is a separate, narrower diagnostic — and its T2 is FP64-vs-FP64

`f3b_laneequal_verdict.json` (separate follow-up, PASS on its own tripwires):

- T1 translation invariance: |dE| = 4.1382008930668235e-11 on BOTH lanes
  (band 5e-9). Genuine diagnostic pass; a magnitude-dependent fp32-VV10 origin
  kernel defect would have breached it.
- T2 rotation lane-equality: dE_rot(auto) == dE_rot(fp64) bitwise
  (5.437911681838159e-06, lane_diff = 0.0).

Audit-verified fact from the raw counters (this erratum, 2026-10-30):
aspirin/def2-SVP is **222 AO**, under the 250-AO SP-14/SP-16 size gate, so the
auto lane ran **bitwise FP64 by design** — `precision_switch.switched=False`
and `fp32_entry_hits` = 0 in every category (df_jk / eval_rho / xc_grid /
grad_ip1 / other) in `f3_aspirin_orig.json`, `f3_aspirin_rot.json`,
`f3b_orig_auto.json`, `f3b_rot_auto.json`; the auto and fp64 energies agree in
every digit on all geometries. Therefore:

- T2 is an **FP64-vs-FP64 identity**, not a mixed-precision comparison. It is
  NOT evidence about rotation response of an actual FP32 mixed-precision
  trajectory (none existed on this cell).
- T2's valid meaning is only: the measured rotation response is produced by
  the FP64 lane itself and is shared by both lanes by construction.

F3b does NOT retrospectively satisfy or replace the original F3
rotation-invariance contract.
